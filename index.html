ABOUT US

Founded in 2021 by husband-and-wife duo Engr. Kenneth and Joan Ballao, KAI Aluminum & Glass Supply is a Bataan-based provider of premium aluminum and glass solutions for both residential and commercial projects.

Our branding is inspired by the cube, a symbol of structure, strength, and balance. The height represents our ambition for growth, the width reflects our expanding reach across the country, and the depth captures our unwavering dedication to craftsmanship.
